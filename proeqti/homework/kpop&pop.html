KPOP AND POP


K Pop

  • Originated in South Korea
  • Known for choreography & visuals
  • Groups often have many members
  • Mixes genres (hip-hop, EDM, R&B)
  • Strong fandom culture

Pop

  • Originated mainly in the U.S./UK
  • Focus on catchy melodies
  • Mostly solo artists
  • Radio-friendly production
  • Broad global audience

Artists

Some of the most popular artists are: BLACKPINK, BIGBANG, TWICE, Stray Kids, SEVENTEEN, NCT, 2NE1, NewJeans, LOONA, Ive, LE SSERAFIM, ATEEZ, TXT, ENHYPEN, Red Velvet.

Some of the most popular pop artists are: Michael Jackson, Beyoncé, Lana del ray, Rihanna, Lady Gaga, Shakira, Doja Cat, Ariana Grande, The Weeknd, Ed Sheeran, Billie Eilish, Justin beiber.
